FOR SALE BY PRIVATE TREATY

Attractive c.0.79-Acre Site at Milleenawillin, Skibbereen, Co. Cork

Offered Subject to Planning Permission

Guiding Price: €79,999

Irish Rural Homes is pleased to offer for sale this attractive rural site extending to approximately 0.32 hectares (0.79 acres).

The proposed site occupies a pleasant countryside position at Milleenawillin, within convenient reach of Skibbereen and the surrounding West Cork countryside.

Positioned beside a local road junction, the site enjoys an attractive rural outlook over the surrounding fields and countryside.

Skibbereen offers an extensive selection of shops, supermarkets, schools, healthcare facilities, professional services, cafés, restaurants and sporting amenities.

The surrounding West Cork area is renowned for its attractive countryside, coastal scenery and convenient access to a wide range of recreational and marine amenities.

This rural site provides an opportunity for a purchaser seeking to construct their own home, subject to obtaining the necessary planning permission and satisfying all applicable planning requirements.

The property is being offered subject to the purchaser obtaining planning permission. Rural housing eligibility and other planning requirements may apply. Interested parties should satisfy themselves regarding their planning eligibility and the suitability of the property for their proposed development.

The stated site area and boundaries are approximate and remain subject to final agreement, mapping and confirmation by the vendor’s solicitor and a suitably qualified surveyor.

Maps and further particulars are available from Irish Rural Homes.

OFFERS AND PURCHASER SELECTION

All offers will be considered on their overall merits. The vendor reserves the right to select the preferred purchaser and is not obliged to accept the highest or any offer.

As the property is being offered subject to planning permission, prospective purchasers may be requested to provide evidence of funds and preliminary written advice from a suitably qualified architect or planning consultant indicating that they appear capable of satisfying the applicable rural housing and planning criteria.

No pre-planning request or formal planning application may be submitted in respect of the property without the vendor’s prior written consent. Any proposed planning application must also comply with the terms agreed through the vendor’s solicitor.

No assurance or warranty is given that planning permission will be granted.
